# V4.1-A retirement runbook (ADR-0041)

**Date:** 2026-06-27. **Owner:** PM (drives read-only + comms); operator runs key-bearing txs. **Decision:** ADR-0041 (collapse to one vault). Operator confirmed 2026-06-27: retire A, keep B, withdraw-only, **no forced migration**.

## Target & live state (read 2026-06-26/27, mainnet chain 143)
- **Retire:** V4.1-A `0x933FF608eaC2b3221088bd9AE19b05F266dBF7DA` — round 3, **state = Sales (open)**, sales end ~`1782484627`, **1 ticket / 1 MON principal**. Not paused.
- **Keep:** V4.1-B `0x1886f329e486e934c76028B15a580850e74d404C` — round 9, healthy, the single visible beta vault.
- Why A: newer, smaller, round 3 vs 9, and currently only **one** depositor at risk of any confusion — lowest-impact to retire.

## Guiding invariants (no-loss; don't strand a prize)
1. A's depositors must **always be able to withdraw principal.** V4.1 keeps withdrawals live even when paused (verified).
2. **Never hide A while a prize is mid-flight.** A is in Sales with 1 ticket — there is **no locked/accruing prize yet**, so this is the cleanest possible moment.
3. Pool-config changes (frontend `VITE_POOL_ADDRESSES`, indexer `POOL_ADDRESSES`) go through the canonical reconciliation control (backlog P0-1) so B is never accidentally dropped and A can't silently re-drift back.

## Recommended sequence & timing

**Step 0 — decide A's single open ticket (now).** A has 1 ticket (1 MON) in an open sales window. Two clean options:
- **0a (fastest):** pause A now → sales stop; tell that one depositor their 1 MON is withdrawable; they withdraw. Round 3 never locks. Done in one tx + one DM.
- **0b (most courteous):** let round 3 run its normal cycle (lock → draw → settle, ~the V4.1 round+lock window) so the existing ticket gets its shot at the (tiny) prize, **then** retire after settle.
**Recommendation: 0a.** The prize on 1 MON over a lock period is negligible, and dragging the wind-down across a full round just delays the UX simplification users are asking for. Refund-by-withdraw is cleaner and immediate.

**Step 1 — stop new deposits (operator, key-bearing).** Pause A (`pause()` from the owner/guardian key). This halts new tickets; withdrawals stay live. *I verify on-chain `paused()==true` and that a withdraw simulation succeeds.*

**Step 2 — remove A from the live surfaces (operator).**
- Frontend: drop A from Vercel `VITE_POOL_ADDRESSES` (keep B). Redeploy.
- Indexer: drop A from `POOL_ADDRESSES` (keep B + current V4-B), via the reconciliation control, not a hand-set secret.
- *I verify on the live surfaces (working rule 6): the app shows only B; the indexer no longer serves A's rounds; B still resolves.*

**Step 3 — surface a withdraw path for A (operator + me).** A is hidden from the main flow but its 1 depositor must still be able to exit. Minimum: a documented "withdraw from a retired vault" path (direct contract call instructions or a hidden UI route). *I confirm a withdraw still succeeds against paused A.*

**Step 4 — comms (operator, with my draft).** DM/notice to A's depositor(s): "Vault A is being retired to simplify EverDraw to a single prize pool. Your principal is 100% safe and withdrawable now; please withdraw and (optionally) join Vault B." I'll draft the exact copy. **Hiding A from the UI without this comms is a trust problem — do not skip.**

**Step 5 — done for V4.1.** One visible vault (B). A is paused, withdraw-only, off the indexer/frontend.
