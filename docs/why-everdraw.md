# Why EverDraw

## DeFi has a retention problem on both sides

Users are disengaged. They chase yields, farm airdrops, and leave. Prediction markets are pulling them out of DeFi entirely because those products offer real excitement, but at the cost of capital. Users end up poorer and more checked out over time.

Protocols are wasting growth budgets. Airdrops produce 90% farmer churn. Liquidity mining attracts mercenary capital that exits the moment incentives stop. Points programs are running into fatigue.

EverDraw addresses both with one mechanism.

---

## For users: excitement without risk

Prize linked savings has decades of behavioural economics behind it. Studies consistently show 30 to 40 percent higher deposit rates than equivalent interest accounts. People will choose a chance to win a large prize over the certainty of a small return, even when the expected value is identical.

Traditional lotteries exploit this destructively. Players lose money. The house wins.

EverDraw redirects the same instinct toward saving. The prize comes from staking yield, not from anyone else's losses. Nobody funds the pot by losing. The staking layer pays for it. That is the distinction. EverDraw aligns with the same psychology lotteries exploit and points it at positive financial behaviour.

> "Deposit MON. Maybe win the pot. Always keep your lot."

No liquidation risk. No impermanent loss. No positions to manage.

---

## For protocols: a better retention tool

Every Monad protocol with a growth budget faces the same question. How do you acquire users who actually stay?

EverDraw's CampaignManager (Phase 2) gives a new option. Instead of airdropping tokens that get dumped, a protocol funds a branded prize campaign. Users participate for the chance to win. They come back weekly. Winners post about it. The protocol gets recurring engagement and measurable retention.

A campaign is funded with a single treasury transfer. No contract changes on the protocol's side. No audit. EverDraw runs draw execution, winner selection, claim flows, and frontend integration. The protocol keeps its branding and user relationship.

This is structurally different from airdrops, points, or liquidity mining. Those create one shot extraction. Prize campaigns create recurring engagement loops.

## Built in virality

Every winner is organic marketing. "I just won 5,000 MON on EverDraw" is a tweet, a Discord flex, a reason for someone in their network to try it. That is structural to how prize savings works. Winners want to tell people they won. No protocol pays for the content. It happens every draw.

## Why now, why Monad

Three things have converged to make EverDraw possible.

**Native, reliable yield via shMON.** Previous prize savings designs had to source yield from lending markets, which are cyclical and unreliable. shMON yield is consensus layer yield, tied to Monad itself. It exists for as long as Monad runs. EverDraw has a yield floor that does not go to zero.

**Monad's performance unlocks the UX.** A 1 MON ticket does not work when gas is 5 dollars. Weekly draws are not exciting when settlement takes minutes. Monad's throughput, sub second finality, and near zero gas costs make micro entries economically viable.

**Protocol incentive models are exhausted.** The gap between what users want (excitement with safety) and what DeFi offers (complexity with risk) has never been wider. Protocols are actively searching for retention tools that beat the airdrop and points playbook. EverDraw arrives at the moment both sides need it most.
