# The ShMON Partnership

ShMON is the yield layer behind EverDraw's MON vault.

When users deposit MON into EverDraw, the product uses ShMON as the underlying yield source. That yield is what funds the round prize.

---

## Why that matters

**Native to Monad**

The yield is tied to Monad's staking economy.

**Simple for users**

Users interact with EverDraw through MON deposits and round actions. They do not need to manage the underlying staking mechanics inside EverDraw itself.

**Aligned incentives**

EverDraw and ShMON are aligned because the product depends on real Monad-native yield rather than temporary incentive emissions.
