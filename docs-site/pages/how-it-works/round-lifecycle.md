# Round Lifecycle

Every EverDraw round moves through four simple stages.

---

## 1. Deposit

**Duration:** 24 hours

The active vault is open for deposits.

You can buy tickets during this window. Your deposit is committed to that round until it finishes.

**What you can do:**
- Buy tickets
- Make multiple deposits in the same round

**What you cannot do:**
- Withdraw during the open deposit window

---

## 2. Yield Accumulating

**Duration:** about 7 days

Once deposits close, the vault locks and the round continues generating yield.

No more tickets can be added to that round. During this stage, the round is simply running.

**What you can do:**
- Wait for the draw
- Deposit into the next active round if one is open

---

## 3. Winner Revealed

**When it happens:** at the end of the round

When the round finishes, EverDraw reveals the winner in the UI.

You can view:
- the winning wallet
- the winning ticket
- the participant leaderboard
- the prize amount shown for the round

---

## 4. Claim / Withdraw

After the round is finalized, funds are available.

**Winners can:**
- Claim the prize
- Withdraw principal

**Everyone else can:**
- Withdraw principal

These actions are available from the Previous Vault view in the app.

---

## Round summary

| Stage | Duration | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit | 24 hours | Tickets are available |
| Yield Accumulating | ~7 days | Yield builds during the round |
| Winner Revealed | End of round | Winner and results appear |
| Claim / Withdraw | After finalization | Prize claim and principal withdrawal are available |

---

## Notes

- If a round receives no deposits, it can be skipped and a new round opens.
- Your chance of winning is based on your tickets as a share of total tickets in that round.
