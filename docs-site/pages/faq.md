# FAQ

**Can I lose my deposit?**

No. EverDraw is designed so user principal is returned after the round is finalized. The prize comes from yield, not from other users losing money.

---

**How is the prize funded?**

The prize comes from yield generated during the round.

---

**What is my chance of winning?**

Your chance of winning is your tickets divided by the total tickets in the round.

---

**How long does a round last?**

A round has a 24 hour deposit window followed by a yield period of about 7 days. After the winner is revealed, the round is finalized and claim / withdraw becomes available.

---

**What if no one buys tickets in a round?**

The round can be skipped and a new one opens normally.

---

**What happens to unclaimed prizes?**

If a prize is not claimed within the claim window shown in the UI, it can roll forward into a future round. Principal withdrawals do not expire.

---

**Is the draw fair?**

EverDraw records the draw logic on-chain and the result is independently verifiable.

---

**Is EverDraw audited?**

Not yet. See the [Security](security.md) page for the current status.

---

**Can I interact with the contract directly?**

Yes. The contract is public and can be accessed directly by advanced users.

---

**Is there an EverDraw token?**

No.

---

**Why Monad?**

EverDraw is built around Monad's staking economy and ShMON-based yield.

---

**Can protocols run campaigns through EverDraw?**

Yes. EverDraw is being built to support branded prize campaigns for protocols.
