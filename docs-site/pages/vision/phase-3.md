# Phase 3: Multi Asset and Ecosystem

**Timeline: month 6 to 9.**

Phase 3 expands EverDraw beyond MON into new asset types and new forms of cross protocol collaboration. Prize savings stops being a single token product and becomes a multi asset ecosystem.

---

## Stablecoin Vaults

Prize savings is not limited to one token. Stablecoin vaults bring an entirely new user segment: users who want the excitement of prize savings without exposure to volatile assets.

Deposit USDC or USDT. Earn prize chances. Prizes paid in stables. Principal returned in stables. Same no loss mechanics, applied to assets risk averse users already hold.

Each vault is independent. Its own asset, draw, and winner. MON vaults and stablecoin vaults run simultaneously, serving different users on the same infrastructure.

---

## Cross Protocol Mega Draws

Because EverDraw is a neutral prize layer, multiple protocols can co fund a single massive prize pool.

Imagine a "Monad Season Prize" where several major protocols each contribute. Every user who staked, swapped, lent, or LP'd across any participating protocol is eligible. One draw. One massive winner. Every contributing protocol gets attribution.

No single protocol can build this alone. It needs a trusted neutral layer that sits across the ecosystem. Monad's community is tight knit and protocols are collaborative. Cross protocol mega draws are a moat that only a shared prize layer can build, and that moat strengthens with every new protocol partner.

---

## Prize Boost Campaigns

Protocols sponsor additional prizes on top of yield generated pools.

- A protocol sponsors a MON prize boost on the staking vault to attract depositors.
- A lending protocol sponsors a stablecoin prize boost to drive deposits.
- A new project runs a launch campaign with EverDraw as the distribution mechanism.

Protocols fund prizes as a user acquisition channel. Instead of buying ads or paying influencers, they sponsor prize pools. Users participate to win. The protocol gets deposits, visibility, and on chain engagement data.

---

## Points Layer Expansion

The CampaignManager already handles campaign creation, eligibility tracking, and reward distribution. The natural next step is continuous points tracking for all participants, not just winners.

A hybrid model (earn points for every eligible action, plus a chance to win the jackpot) is more engaging than purely linear distribution. Linear drips have no viral moment. A prize draw does.

This positions EverDraw as both a prize engine and a points layer in one protocol. Protocols that run campaigns get measurable ongoing participation through points and shareable viral moments through prize draws.
