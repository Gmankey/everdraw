# Vision

EverDraw is being built to stay simple for users while becoming more useful for protocols.

The direction is straightforward:
- make prize saving smoother for users
- make prize campaigns easier for protocols
- expand the kinds of vaults and campaigns EverDraw can support

This page describes the general direction, not a fixed timeline.

---

## What that means in practice

**For users**
- easier participation
- simpler round flows
- more ways to save with upside

**For protocols**
- branded prize campaigns
- flexible campaign formats
- a reusable prize layer instead of one-off incentive mechanics

---

## Principles

- Keep the user experience simple
- Keep principal protection clear
- Make prize mechanics easy to understand
- Give protocols flexible campaign tools without adding friction for users
