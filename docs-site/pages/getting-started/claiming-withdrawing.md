# Claiming & Withdrawing

Once a round settles (after the yield period of ~7 days plus the ShMON unstaking phase of 18-24 hours), all participants can action their funds from the **Previous Vault** tab.

---

## The unstaking phase

After the 7-day yield period, the winner is revealed and a countdown begins. During this 18-24 hour ShMON unstaking phase, the winner and results are fully visible in the UI — but claim and withdraw buttons are disabled with a countdown timer. Once the countdown reaches zero, both actions activate.

This is a ShMON protocol constraint, not an EverDraw design choice.

---

## If you won

**Step 1 — Claim your prize**

On the Previous Vault tab, your address will appear in the winner spotlight. Click "Claim Prize" and confirm the transaction. This sends the yield prize to your wallet.

**Step 2 — Withdraw your principal**

After claiming, click "Withdraw Tokens" to return your original MON deposit. This is a separate transaction.

You must claim the prize before it expires. Unclaimed prizes roll into the next round's prize pool.

---

## If you didn't win

Click "Withdraw Tokens" on the Previous Vault tab and confirm the transaction. Your exact MON principal is returned — no slippage, no fees, no loss regardless of the outcome.

---

## Timing

The "Withdraw Tokens" button is disabled with a countdown during the Unstaking ShMON phase. This countdown reflects the remaining time in ShMON's epoch-based unstaking queue and is not configurable.

Once the countdown reaches zero, the button activates and withdrawal is available at any time with no deadline.

---

## There is no penalty for not acting immediately

Settled funds sit in the contract indefinitely until claimed. There is no expiry on principal withdrawals. Take your time.

Prize claims do have an expiry window — unclaimed prizes eventually roll forward to seed the next prize pool. The UI displays the claim deadline clearly.
