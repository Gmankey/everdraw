# For Protocols

EverDraw can power prize campaigns for Monad protocols.

The idea is simple: instead of relying only on one-off incentives, a protocol can run a prize campaign that gives users a reason to come back and participate again.

---

## Why use EverDraw

**Simple for users**

Prize campaigns are easy to understand. Users know what they are doing and what they might win.

**Repeatable**

A campaign format can run more than once and stay familiar.

**Branded**

Protocols can keep their own brand and user relationship while EverDraw handles the prize mechanics underneath.

---

## What EverDraw provides

EverDraw is being built to support:
- campaign funding
- draw logic
- winner selection
- claim flows
- campaign presentation in the user interface

---

## What protocols get

- a simpler incentive format
- a campaign users can understand quickly
- a reusable prize layer instead of rebuilding prize mechanics from scratch
